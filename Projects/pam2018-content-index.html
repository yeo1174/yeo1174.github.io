Key References

7 schedules ▾
Contract Documents (Cl. 3.1) Priority: 1 Agreement · 2 Contract Particulars (Appendix) · 3 Bills of Quantities · 4 Drawings · 5 Specification · 6 Other documents listed in Appendix. BQ included — key difference from the Without Quantities form.
Liquidated Damages (Cl. 22) Rate as stated in Appendix (typical 0.5% – 1% per week, cap ~10% of Contract Sum). Architect issues Certificate of Non-Completion; Employer deducts from monies due with written notice; LAD not subject to set-off or adjudication.
Relevant Events — EOT (Cl. 23.8) Force majeure · exceptionally adverse weather · loss/damage by Insured Risks · Architect's instructions (variations, postponement) · Employer's failure to give possession/information · delay by Nominated Sub-Contractors · delayed possession of Site · civil commotion/strike · statutory authority delays · archaeological finds · Employer's direct contractors · delay in permits/approvals.
Payment Cycle (Cl. 30) Monthly claim by 25th → Interim Certificate within 30 days of valuation → payment within 30 days of certificate. Total payment period ~60 days from valuation. Suspension right after 30 days' non-payment (Cl. 30.7).
Retention (Cl. 30.5 – 30.6) 5% deducted from interim certificates (typical) · 50% released at Practical Completion · remaining 50% released at Certificate of Making Good Defects.
Insurance (Cl. 19 – 20) Third Party Liability · SOCSO for local workmen · Workmen's Compensation for foreign workers · CAR Insurance for new works (Contractor or Employer) · existing structure cover for renovation works. Placed with licensed insurers, Employer approval required.
Dispute Resolution (Cl. 34 – 37) Negotiation → Mediation (Cl. 34, voluntary, PAM rules) → Expert Determination (Cl. 35, optional) → Adjudication (Cl. 36, mandatory for set-off, AIAC rules, decision within 42 days, binding pending arbitration) → Arbitration (Cl. 37, AIAC, Arbitration Act 2005, final and binding).
